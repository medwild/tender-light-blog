Engagement photo idea questions

How many engagement photo ideas should we plan for one session?

Plan 8–12 starting ideas for a 60-minute session. Each idea produces 3–5 keeper frames once you add movement and in-between moments, so 10 ideas easily fills a full gallery without feeling rushed.

What's the most flattering engagement photo idea for camera-shy couples?

Walking frames. Give yourselves somewhere to go — a path, a bridge, a café window — and the camera catches you moving instead of performing. It's the single easiest idea for couples who hate posing.

Should our engagement photo ideas match our wedding theme?

Echo it, don't replicate it. If your wedding is sage-and-cream, a dusty-blue engagement palette gives you range across both galleries instead of one repeated look.
