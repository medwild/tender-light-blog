What to Wear for Engagement Photos

Wear what you'd choose for a nice dinner, in soft muted tones that keep the eye on your faces: cream, oat, terracotta, sage. Coordinate palettes rather than matching outfits — the 70/20/10 rule (70% shared neutral, 20% accent, 10% texture) is the fastest way to look put-together without looking twinned.

Harper's field note: I've never once regretted a couple in muted tones. I've regretted neon, logos and tiny patterns every single time.

Outfit questions

What colors should we wear for engagement photos?

Soft, muted tones flatter every skin tone and photograph warmly: cream, oat, camel, sage, dusty blue, terracotta. Avoid neon, big logos and tiny tight patterns, which date a photo or moiré on camera.

Should we match outfits for engagement photos?

Coordinate, don't match. Share a neutral base and one accent color between you, then add texture. Matching outfits read like a uniform; coordinating outfits read like a life together.

When should we finalize our engagement outfits?

Two weeks before the session, with a mirror photo test in similar light. Send the mirror shots to your photographer — most will happily veto a piece that fights the location.
