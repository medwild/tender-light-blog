Outdoor session questions

What's the best time of day for outdoor engagement photos?

The last 60–90 minutes before sunset for warm, dramatic light. Sunrise gives softer tones and empty locations — worth the alarm if you hate crowds.

What happens if it rains on our outdoor session?

Reschedule, or lean in — umbrella frames and after-rain reflections are keeper material. Overcast is a giant softbox, so a grey sky is a feature, not a cancellation.

Do we need permits for outdoor engagement photos?

For a couple and one photographer, almost never. Tripods, assistants or commercial styling can trigger permits in city parks, and state parks charge per-vehicle entry. Verify the specific spot a week ahead.
