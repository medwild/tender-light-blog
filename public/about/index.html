nice to meet you

I traded the camera for the keyboard

I'm Harper Ellis — engagement photographer for eight years, 400+ sessions in the rearview, now the writer behind this journal. I'm the friend who'll tell you your golden-hour idea is perfect, then quietly steer you away from the spot with the porta-potty. You deserve photos that feel like you — and you don't need to be “good at photos” to get them.

Tender Light started in 2019 with my sister's engagement photos. She kept apologizing for being “awkward,” and I kept thinking: it's not you — nobody told you what to do. This journal is that telling, written down: every pose I directed, every outfit rule I enforced, every location I trusted with someone's golden hour.

These days I live in Austin with my high school sweetheart, writing the guides I wish every couple had. I still scout gravel roads for stone bridges, still argue that grey skies are a feature — I just hand you the words instead of the negatives.

Four rules, no exceptions

  • Direction over posing — give yourselves something to do, not somewhere to freeze. Tasks produce the two seconds after the pose.
  • Natural light, always — no flashes, no studio strobes. Golden hour, open shade, grey-sky softboxes.
  • Timeless over trendy — film-inspired tones that will look as right in 2046 as they do today.
  • You, not a catalogue — every session starts with how you two actually are: the jokes, the fidgets, the quiet habits.

Based in Austin, TX. I consult with couples on their engagement shoot (poses, outfits, locations, photographer shortlists) and partner with wedding brands on honest, tested recommendations. Currently booking spring & summer 2026 — say hello or start with the journal.
