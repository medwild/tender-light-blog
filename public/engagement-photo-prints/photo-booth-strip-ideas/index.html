12 Photo Booth Strip Ideas That Make Your Session Playful

April 4, 2026 · 4 min read · By Harper Ellis

A photo booth strip distills your whole session into four frames and one vertical strip — and it's the artifact guests actually pocket. You don't need a booth to make one: any photographer can shoot a four-frame sequence, and any print service can lay it out as a strip.

The Four-Frame Recipe

The best strips tell a tiny story in four beats. Our go-to sequence: a hello, a kiss, a laugh, and a silly face. The contrast between the sweet and the goofy is what makes people smile every time.

  • Frame 1 — the classic: faces together, smiling at camera.
  • Frame 2 — the kiss.
  • Frame 3 — the laugh, caught mid-motion.
  • Frame 4 — the silly face or the dramatic dip.

Posing for the Strip

Keep all four frames head-and-shoulders tight — booth crops are close, and full-body poses disappear in the narrow format. Face the camera squarely, stay centered, and treat each frame like a one-second beat.

Where Strips Earn Their Keep

  • Tucked into save-the-date envelopes as a surprise insert.
  • Guest favors at the engagement party or wedding.
  • A set for each pair of parents, dated on the back.
  • Framed in a slim vertical frame for a bedside table.
  • A spare strip for your wallet or planner cover.

Harper's tip: Ask for the strip in both color and black-and-white — the B&W looks timeless in a frame, the color feels like the day.

Photo Strip Questions, Answered

Can I make a photo booth strip without an actual booth?

Yes — any photographer can shoot a four-frame sequence, and most print services lay it out as a booth-style strip.

How many frames fit on a photo strip?

The classic strip is four vertical frames, about 2x6 inches.

What should we wear for a photobooth-style strip?

Solid, medium-toned tops photograph cleanest in the tight crop. Avoid busy patterns.
