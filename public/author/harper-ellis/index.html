Harper Ellis

Founder & Engagement Photo Editor · Austin, TX · @tenderlight on Pinterest

Harper spent eight years behind the lens shooting more than 400 engagement sessions across the United States before trading her camera for a keyboard. Based in Austin and married to her high school sweetheart, she founded Tender Light to write the warm, practical guides she wishes every couple had before their shoot.

She believes the best engagement photos are the ones where you forgot the camera was there — and that any couple, however camera-shy, can get them with the right direction. Her guides cover poses, outfits, locations, light, save-the-dates and what to do with the prints afterwards.
