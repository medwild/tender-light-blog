Save the Date Photoshoot Ideas

The best save the date photos come from a casual engagement session — warm, unstiff frames that fit a card layout and read as instantly you. Ask your photographer for a horizontal crop for cards and a square crop for your wedding website, and leave generous negative space where your names and date will sit.
