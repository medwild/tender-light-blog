Engagement Photo Print Ideas

Print the frames you actually live with, not just the formal ones: a large hero print for the wall, mid-size pairings for a gallery ledge, and small candid prints for shelves and desks. Matte or lustre finishes flatter golden-hour light better than glossy, and printing in pairs (one close, one wide) keeps a display from feeling flat.
