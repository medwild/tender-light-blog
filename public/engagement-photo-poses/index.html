Engagement pose questions

What are the easiest engagement photo poses for couples who hate posing?

The forehead touch with eyes closed, walking away hand-in-hand, and the waist-hold mid-laugh. All three remove the pressure of performing for the lens and usually unlock the rest of the session.

How long should we hold an engagement pose?

About 10 seconds. The first 3 are stiff, the next 7 are gold. Your photographer should shoot the whole sequence, not just the setup, because the settle-in frames are the keepers.

What do we do with our hands in engagement poses?

Give them a job: a lapel, a waist, interlaced fingers, a coffee cup, a bouquet. Empty hands look awkward; busy hands look natural. A good photographer directs this explicitly.
