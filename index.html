Engagement Photo Ideas, Poses & Inspiration for Couples

Welcome to Tender Light — the journal I wish every newly-engaged couple had bookmarked before their shoot. I'm Harper Ellis, and for eight years I photographed engagements across the country: more than 400 sessions, thousands of frames, and one lesson I keep re-learning. The photos you'll love in twenty years aren't the stiff, perfect ones. They're the mid-laugh glances, the hand on a collar, the walk where you forgot the camera existed. Everything here is built from that archive: six topic hubs covering ideas, poses, outfits, locations, save-the-dates and prints — written for couples, not models. Start anywhere. You two are already more photogenic than you believe.
