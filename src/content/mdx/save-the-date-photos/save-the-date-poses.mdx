A save-the-date is a photograph with a job: it has to carry your names, your date, and your
venue without feeling crowded. So the best **save the date poses** aren't the tightest or the
most romantic — they're the ones that leave room for your name.

## Walking Frames With Room to Breathe

Walking toward camera, positioned in the left third — sky fills the right. That empty third is
where your date goes. Every walking frame should be composed with the card in mind.

> The best save the date photo isn't the best photo of you — it's the best photo of you with
> room for your name.

## Seated & Still Frames

A seated pose on a ledge or blanket, off-center, with the empty side reserved for your date.
Stillness reads well on a card because it doesn't compete with the typography.

## Wide & Environmental Frames

Tiny-couple-big-landscape: you small at the base, the landscape carrying the card. These are the
frames designers reach for first.
