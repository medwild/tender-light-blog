Fall is the most photogenic season there is — warm, directional light, golden foliage, and a
palette that flatters every skin tone. But it only works if you dress for it. **What to wear for
fall pictures** comes down to earth tones, real texture, and layers that look as good as they feel.

## The Fall Color Palette That Works

The colors that glow against autumn foliage are the earth tones one step removed from it — rust
instead of pumpkin, olive instead of leaf-green, camel instead of tan.

- Rust, camel, olive, mustard, deep green.
- Neutrals to anchor: cream, oat, denim, charcoal.
- One accent per couple, not per person.

## Textures & Layers That Photograph Rich

Chunky cable knits, wool coats, corduroy, flannel. Texture is what makes fall frames feel rich —
smooth, flat fabrics disappear in golden light; knits and wool catch it.
